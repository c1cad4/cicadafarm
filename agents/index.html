Ordering and payment

  • Goods keep a fixed USD price. Payment uses CICADAFARM tokens at the live GeckoTerminal pool rate. Token quantities are not fixed; fetch a fresh rate.
  • Honey: $120 per litre, 100 litres published stock. Worldwide postal delivery is quoted separately. Eggs: $1 each, 100 available, farm pickup only.
  • Tea: $30/kg, 50 kg published stock. Cedar nuts: $50/kg, 300 kg published stock. Confirm delivery with the farm.
  • Hive rental: $5,000 for two years or $10,000 for three years. Four hives are shared across both terms, not four per term.
  • Contact @robot_cyb to confirm availability and delivery before paying. A transfer does not reserve stock. The site has no central order database or automatic Telegram notifications.
  • The checkout issues a five-minute Solana Pay quote with a unique order memo. A buyer can submit a transaction signature for finalized on-chain checking and download a receipt. The farm independently checks the transaction and confirms fulfilment.

Data quality and privacy

  • Impact figures and seasonal weather are illustrative estimates, not verified production, weather measurements or ecological ratings.
  • Wildlife observations count sightings and tracks, not population sizes or evidence of population growth.
  • Personal observations remain on the visitor’s device. Farm location and sighting coordinates are private and are not included in this catalogue.
